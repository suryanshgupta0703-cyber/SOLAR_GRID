1. THE BIG IDEA (WHAT IS THIS PROJECT IN SIMPLE WORDS?)
================================================================================
Imagine your housing society has rooftop solar panels.

On a bright sunny afternoon, your solar panels generate a huge amount of free
electricity. But at 1:00 PM, most residents are away at work or school, so almost
nobody is using power. 

Meanwhile, heavy machines—like the society's water pump and EV car chargers—are
turned on at 7:00 in the morning or 8:00 at night. At those hours, the sun is down,
and the electricity company charges their highest peak rates!

SOLARGRID is a smart computer brain for the housing society:
1. It checks tomorrow's weather forecast.
2. It predicts exactly how much solar power the roof will produce each hour.
3. It predicts how much electricity the residents will need.
4. It automatically plans the best hours to run the heavy machines (like water
   pumps and EV chargers) so they run on FREE midday solar energy instead of
   expensive grid electricity!

Result: Massive savings on electricity bills without changing anyone's daily life.

2. THE REAL-LIFE PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING
================================================================================
Why do solar societies struggle today?

1. THE SUN DOESN'T CARE ABOUT OUR ROUTINE:
   Solar panels make peak power between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM. Humans use peak power
   at 8:00 AM (breakfast rush) and 8:00 PM (dinner, lights, TV, AC).

2. THE "CHEAP EXPORT, EXPENSIVE IMPORT" TRAP:
   If your society produces extra solar energy at 1:00 PM and sends it back to
   the government grid, the power company only pays you around Rs 2.50 per unit.
   But when you buy electricity from them at night, they charge you up to Rs 8.50
   per unit! Selling cheap and buying expensive is a huge financial loss.

3. GUESSWORK BY SOCIETY GUARDS:
   Today, building caretakers turn on water pumps manually whenever they feel like it,
   often right during expensive morning peak tariff hours.

3. HOW SOLARGRID SOLVES IT (THE 4-STEP SOLUTION)
================================================================================
STEP 1: LOOK AT TOMORROW'S SKY
The system looks at 3 key weather factors for the next 24 hours:
- Sun Intensity (Solar Irradiance in W/m²)
- Cloud Cover (0% = clear sky, 100% = heavy clouds)
- Temperature (panels actually work slightly slower if they get too hot!)

STEP 2: PREDICT HOURLY POWER
Using Machine Learning, the system predicts two curves for tomorrow:
- Solar Curve: How many kilowatts (kW) the panels will make each hour.
- Demand Curve: How many kilowatts the society will consume for base lights & fans.

STEP 3: FIND THE "FREE ENERGY WINDOW"
The system compares the two curves:
- When Solar > Demand, we have a "SURPLUS" (free extra power, usually 10 AM to 3 PM).
- When Solar < Demand, we have a "DEFICIT" (we have to buy from the grid).

STEP 4: AUTOMATICALLY SCHEDULE HEAVY APPLIANCES
The smart scheduler takes the heavy controllable machines and schedules them:
- Water Pump (3.5 kW, needs 2 hours): Runs between 11:00 AM and 1:00 PM.
- EV Charger (7.4 kW, needs 4 hours): Runs between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM.
Both machines run almost 100% on free solar sunshine!

4. THE CORE COMPONENTS EXPLAINED SIMPLY
================================================================================
4.1 THE DATABASE (SQLite):
A simple, zero-setup storage box that saves 30 days of past hourly readings
(720 hours total). It keeps track of past weather, past solar output, and past
colony usage.

4.2 THE TWO AI BRAINS:
To predict tomorrow's solar power accurately, SolarGrid uses a "team" of two
different machine learning models:

A) LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory Neural Network):
   - Think of this as the "Pattern Memory" expert.
   - It is a Deep Learning model that remembers sequences. If 10:00 AM is sunny,
     it knows 11:00 AM is usually sunny too.

B) XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting):
   - Think of this as the "Weather Rules" expert.
   - It is a fast tree-based model that knows exact numbers: "If cloud cover jumps
     from 10% to 70%, drop solar output by 65% immediately."

By combining both (60% XGBoost + 40% LSTM), SolarGrid gets the best of both worlds:
smooth diurnal curves AND sharp reactions to sudden clouds.

4.3 THE HEURISTIC SCHEDULER:
A rule-based algorithm that acts like a smart organizer:
- Rule 1 (Priority): Water pump comes first because clean water is essential.
- Rule 2 (No Interruption): You cannot stop an EV charger midway; once started,
  it finishes its charge block.
- Rule 3 (Grid Cap): Never turn on so many machines at once that the total power
  exceeds what the rooftop panels can handle.

4.4 THE STREAMLIT WEB DASHBOARD:
An interactive webpage with dark-mode styling and clear charts where the society
manager can:
- See tomorrow's solar graph vs. demand graph in one click.
- See the exact hours recommended for each appliance.
- See how much money (in Rupees) and energy (in kWh) the society saved.

5. REAL NUMBERS & RESULTS (HOW WELL DOES IT WORK?)
================================================================================
1. PREDICTION ACCURACY:
   - Solar Power Error: Only 1.34 kW on a 25 kW system (over 90% accuracy).
   - Demand Error: Only 0.91 kW error.

2. ABLATION STUDY (Why both models together matter):
   - Standalone LSTM error: 1.58 kW
   - Standalone XGBoost error: 1.41 kW
   - SolarGrid Hybrid Team error: 1.34 kW (15.2% more accurate than LSTM alone!)

3. FINANCIAL BENEFIT FOR THE SOCIETY:
   - Baseline (No AI): Heavy appliances run in morning/evening -> Cost: ~Rs 185/day
   - SolarGrid Optimized: Heavy appliances shifted to solar hours -> Cost: ~Rs 82/day
   - DAILY SAVINGS: ~Rs 103 per day (~55% bill reduction on scheduled appliances!)
   - OVER A YEAR: Up to Rs 35,000 – Rs 40,000 saved for a single residential block!

7. QUICK VIVA Q&A (CHEAT-SHEET FOR PRESENTATIONS)
================================================================================
Q1: What is the main objective of SolarGrid?
A1: To maximize on-site solar consumption and cut grid bills by predicting tomorrow's
    solar surplus and scheduling heavy community appliances into that window.

Q2: Why use Machine Learning instead of a fixed timer?
A2: A fixed timer turns on machines at 12:00 PM every day even if it rains!
    SolarGrid's AI looks at cloud cover and rainfall forecast to only schedule
    machines when solar power will actually be produced.

Q3: Why did you choose a hybrid of LSTM and XGBoost?
A3: LSTM captures the 24-hour time sequence and smooth daily cycle, while XGBoost
    excels at non-linear weather cutoff rules (like sudden cloudy spells).
    Ensembling them reduced prediction error by 15.2%.

Q4: What is the Time-of-Day (ToD) tariff?
A4: Utility companies charge different rates depending on the hour. They charge
    highest during morning (7–9 AM) and evening (7–10 PM), and lower during the
    day. SolarGrid avoids these expensive peak penalty hours.

Q5: What are the appliances scheduled?
A5: Community water pumps (3.5 kW, 2 hours run time) and residential EV chargers
    (7.4 kW, 4 hours run time).
